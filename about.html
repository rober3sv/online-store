About the Builder

Welcome to Roberts Custom Truck Parts. With a background rooted in mechanical engineering and heavy-duty truck maintenance, I focus on solving common design oversights through custom engineering and precise manufacturing.

Every part featured here is designed, tested, and 3D printed to exact functional tolerances. By integrating multiple independent factory components into streamlined single-piece designs, these components eliminate unnecessary assembly connections and minimize potential failure points in classic automotive setups.
